About

Welcome to my homepage! My full name is Phi Van Dat, you can call me Dat 👋. I am currently an AI Resident at the Center for AI Research, VinUniversity 🤖, where I am supervised by Prof. Dinh Ngoc Thanh and mentored by Dr. Trung Le (Monash University). I am also a research student at the Foundation Model Lab, BKAI, HUST, working on knowledge distillation with Dr. Ngo Van Linh, and an undergraduate student in Computer Science at Hanoi University of Science and Technology.

Research Interests

My research focuses on developing novel methods in Machine Learning, with an emphasis on improving learning algorithms and optimization techniques. I have a primary interest in Preference Learning and Optimization, particularly in developing effective methods for learning from human preferences and designing optimization algorithms. I am eager to explore related research directions.
